About your pay periods

Biweekly periods start Monday, November 16, 2026. Each includes two Monday–Sunday weeks. The work schedule is Monday–Friday, with weekends excluded from automatic minimum-pay projections. You can still manually record weekend work.

Forecasts assume $330 for unentered weekdays; they are planning estimates, not guaranteed payroll totals. Your 71% net rate is also an estimate, not a tax calculation.
